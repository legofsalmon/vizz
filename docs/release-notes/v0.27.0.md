## What's in it

- **Four more clouds that are alive.** **cyclic** is an automaton whose states chase each other round a ring: noise organises itself into scroll waves that turn for ever, because a spiral's own wave comes back round to feed it. **tangle** is one long elastic rod loose in a wind, with its length and its curvature both held as constraints and self-repulsion on a grid, so it cannot pass through itself — which is the difference between a tangle and a scribble. **crystal** is Reiter's snowflake on a hexagonal lattice, where the six-fold symmetry is imposed nowhere and the habit follows the vapour. **vortex** is the thin cores smoke rings are made of, moving each other by Biot–Savart, so rings catch the one in front and thread through it.
