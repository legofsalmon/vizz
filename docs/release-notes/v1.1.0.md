## What's in it

Everything merged since 1.0.0: new ways to draw, about thirty new shapes and simulations, an ST 2110 output, and the panel moved onto the shared design system.

### New ways to draw
- **Lines and streaks.** `/particles/stroke` draws each particle as a line along its path or as a streak of its motion, so flows and curves read as trajectories rather than dots.
- **Small lit solids.** The surface mode can draw each particle as a tetrahedron, cube, octahedron or shard, turned to the way it travels.
- **Fractal solids.** The Mandelbulb, Mandelbox, quaternion Julia and Menger sponge, sphere-traced and lit in the surface mode.
- **Ink.** Outlines, hatching and stipple for the surface mode.
- **Plexus lines** between nearby particles.
- **Liquid.** Melts the surface mode's discs into one glossy surface.
- **Gaussian splats.** Draws particles as sorted splats, and reads splat captures.
- **Haze and light beams.**

### New shapes and simulations
- 4D polytopes, fractal flames, seashells, the Buddhabrot, Kleinian sets, hyperbolic tilings, colonised trees and meshes.
- Differential growth, and an XY oscilloscope that draws the input signal.
- Four simulations after Joëlle Snaith's visuals for Richie Hawtin. Each answers the music at two levels: a groove moves the form, and only a drop breaks it.
  - **bars:** thick bars, one band each.
  - **fold:** an outline that a kick cracks or shatters into shards.
  - **cells:** a Voronoi field that kicks split and quiet merges back.
  - **ridges:** the last two seconds of the spectrum as forty rows of ridges, after the *Unknown Pleasures* sleeve.
- Simulations drawn as lines are now drawn sharp, without the slight scatter that makes a cloud read as a volume.
- Simulations can read a 64-band spectrum. It follows the band gains, so `fit` sets it too.

### Outputs and finish
- **ST 2110.** `--st2110 239.1.1.1:5004` sends the master as uncompressed SMPTE ST 2110-20 video and writes the SDP file a receiver needs. It needs no special network card. Give it twice for an ST 2022-7 pair.
- **Burn.** `/fx/burn` takes the hottest light to white in the graded finish, the way a camera clips an LED wall.
- NDI could send a frame and then the one before it. It now always sends in order.
- **send over NDI** is a checkbox in the outputs section, and it is remembered.

### The panel
- **Shared design system.** Colours, states and motion come from LeTissier's shared design system.
  - The band meters go grey, amber and red.
  - A licence check shows a spinner after a second.
  - An armed MIDI learn says "MIDI: pick a pad".
  - Settings that take effect at once are now switches. Sliders and checkboxes take the system's shapes.
- Clicking a palette name selects it.
- Arrows in panel text draw properly.
- The Documentation link goes to letissier.ie/docs.
- On Windows with no `HOME`, settings go to `.config\vizz` in your profile, and an old `.\vizz` folder is moved there.
