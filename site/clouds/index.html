Clouds from equations

vizz draws point clouds. Most of them come from a scanner, a camera or a file — and 66 of them come from mathematics, with 15 more that are still running while you watch.

Every picture on this page was drawn by the app's own code, from the same functions that fill a slot when you pick one from the menu. Nothing here is an artist's impression of what a generator makes.

To use one: open the clouds section of the panel and pick from generate…, or start vizz with --cloud gen:thomas. A made cloud takes the next free slot and behaves exactly as a scanned one does: chosen by name, crossed to with a scene change, captured by a preset, lit, and remade the next time you launch. The ones with knobs take them after a ? — gen:torus-knot?p=2;q=5. The live ones run in the live slot: --live-cloud sim:fluid, or simulate… beside receive.

Where these came from. Almost none of it is ours. Each entry says whose work it is, and links to somewhere to read about it where there is somewhere worth sending you. If we have credited something wrongly, that is a bug like any other — please tell us.

flows

Differential equations, integrated from one point on the attractor and recorded in time order. Consecutive points are consecutive moments, which is what makes the cloud crawl along itself rather than shimmer.

maps

Iterated rather than integrated: a rule applied over and over to a point in a plane, lifted into depth by delay embedding — Takens' theorem says a delayed coordinate unfolds the dynamics rather than merely decorating them.

searched

Nobody chose these. Thirty coefficients are drawn at random and the result is kept only if it is chaotic, which about one draw in a few hundred is. The seed is the whole parameter, so the same seed is the same attractor on every machine.

surfaces

Swept in scan order over two parameters, so the cloud reads as a surface rather than a fog.

curves

Traced along a curve and thickened into a tube, in order, so the crawl runs along the curve.

fractals

Self-similar at every scale, found by the chaos game, by escape time, or by marching rays inward until they stop escaping.

grown

Built step by step by a rule, rather than evaluated: rewriting an alphabet, or letting particles wander in and stick.

patterns

Sampled where a pattern is, rather than swept: a point is kept if the field says so.

15 that are alive

These are not made once. Each runs on a thread of its own at the frame rate and streams into the live slot, driven by whatever the audio input is doing: the four bands, the loudness, and where the bar is. That is the whole interface they get — deliberately, because the point is a cloud that happens to be alive rather than a second engine with its own controls.

The plates below are each one caught a couple of seconds in, with no audio playing. The ones that are about growing were given longer.

fields

A grid of numbers stepped forward sixty times a second. The cloud is what rides it.

many bodies

Many things, each with a position of its own, and rules about how they see each other.
